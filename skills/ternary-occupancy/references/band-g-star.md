# Band template: めちゃくちゃ良い \(G^\star\)

Orchestrator translates this 4-axis template into one worktree-local contract. The generation unit reads that contract only — not this file together with the other two templates.

Official name: **めちゃくちゃ良い**. Do not print the name on the after.

## You may

| Axis | Allowed work |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | Pick **exactly one** of the 7 face dimensions as the thesis. Repeat that new principle or new token family in ≥ 2 places. Leave the other face dimensions unchanged, or at most local. |
| **Risk** | One new decision inside existing hard constraints (tokens you may extend, a11y, brand). Template beauty (purple wash, generic glass cards, pointless blobs, silent Inter swap) is \(F\), not a thesis. |
| **Reversibility** | Adding one principle is allowed. Reverting it should remove the face. |
| **Surprise** | The explanation is “what we decided.” A viewer can name the decision in 3 seconds. |

## Face contract

- Thesis count = number of 決断 dimensions. Must be **exactly 1**
- Two nameable theses is \(F\)
- Introducing a table rule that had 0 before applications, with ≥ 2 repetitions, is a decision — that may be this band’s thesis
- One-step color is never a decision, even with ≥ 2 repetitions
- Do not break floor clauses 1, 3–7 or clause 2 for “beauty”

## Floor (copy into the contract verbatim)

Floor = clauses 1, 3–7. Clause 2 stays in the generation check. No band is exempt. Do not add a second styling system.
